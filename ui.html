Test an absence

Everyone is tested individually. Add an optional pair by entering two exact person IDs from your availability file.

Assumptions and file format

One person can cover one role per shift. Shifts are assumed not to overlap. Each covered dependent role requires a covered supervisor role in the same shift. Candidate lists alone do not prove feasibility. Training options are assessed one at a time. Scores are not probabilities or fairness measures. See README for CSV headers and limitations.
